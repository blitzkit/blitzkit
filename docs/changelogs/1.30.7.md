# BlitzKit 1.30.7

Thank you to [Mark Greene](https://github.com/Hitjack007) for your contributions this update.

## Features

- Dragging items in the Tier List now automatically scrolls the screen if you reach the top/bottom edge of the screen

## Fixes

- Clicking on the armor multiple times with HE no longer crashes BlitzKit [Mark Greene]
- Ricochets that later intersect with external modules no longer falsely claim a successful damaging penetration [Mark Greene]
- The lighting animation no longer stops prematurely when BlitzKit is unfocused
- The spot light angle has been increased to more evenly light up larger tanks
- BlitzKit once again uses soft shadows, reducing the pixelated shadows
- Fixed the weird patterns that formed on the tracks of tanks under self-shadows
- Increased the floor size and light decay to create the illusion that the floor is infinite
- Safari no longer crashes when unloading textures for unloaded tanks [Mark Greene]
- The gun depression transition between two constraints (like often behind tanks) now uses a gentler zoned and can never be 0
- Armor plate names in Static Armor are now properly capitalized in English and translated to other languages [Mark Greene]
